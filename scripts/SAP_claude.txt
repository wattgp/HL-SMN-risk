* General rules*

*Objectives*:

1. Estimate the cumulative incidence of subsequent malignant neoplasms (SMNs), overall and by specific cancer site for a population of Hodgkin Lymphoma survivors
2. For individual cancer sites with at leat 50 incident cases, assess the association with treatment exposures: doxorubicin equivalent dose, radiation dose to the affected site, and procarbazine equivalent dose. Of course sex-specific cancers (breast, prostate, cervix, etc.) should be restricted to the appropriate sex. 

3. Evaluate whether the incidence of each cancer site has changed during different
treatment eras

This is thus primarily an etiologic study and the final result should emphasize
interpretability over maximum predictive descrimination.

*Dataset overview*:

The data sample used in this study comes from a study of individuals treated for Hodgkin Lymphoma. The present study is limited to those treated from 1989 till the most recent year. You will be working from a dummy dataset that doesn't contain the actual dates or any patient information ("DUMMY_.csv""). I will run the final script on the actual data.
